Our Story

Not American pizza.
Italian, for real.

Puccettoni was born from a simple conviction: South Florida deserved Italian food made the genuine way — handcrafted dough, imported ingredients, and recipes that carry the old country in them.

Every pizza, focaccia and panzerotto is made in-house by Chef Marco, who treats his craft the way Italians always have: as a matter of family pride.
