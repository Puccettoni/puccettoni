Our Roots

Not American pizza. The real thing.

Puccettoni was born from a simple conviction: South Florida deserved Italian food made the genuine way — handcrafted dough, real ingredients, and recipes that carry the old country in them.

It isn't tied to a single style. Roman, Neapolitan, Sicilian rustic — they all live on the menu, because the through-line was never one shape of pizza. It's authentic artisan Italian, made with care.

The Craft

Handmade by Chef Marco

Every pizza, focaccia, panzerotto and plate of gnocchi is made in-house by Chef Marco, who treats his craft the way Italians always have — as a matter of family pride.

Fresh buffalo mozzarella, focaccia sandwiches, panzerotti, truffle, and Italian pastries: one authentic Italian experience, served the way it's meant to be.
